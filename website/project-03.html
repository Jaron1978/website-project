Azure AI Service Desk Simulator

A planned, fictional IT support environment that will explore practical Azure AI services alongside AI-901 study. It will simulate safe first-line triage, grounded self-service guidance and the operational view of a modern support queue.

AzureAIIT OperationsResponsible AI

Project status

AAI-01 — Project foundation is in progress. The initial scope, safety boundaries, architecture direction, cost plan, testing strategy and 30-card delivery plan are now being documented.

This is a fictional simulation only. It will use made-up people, assets, tickets and operational data — never employer or customer information. No Azure resources have been created yet.

Project Delivery Board

The project follows the same transparent delivery approach as RonBot. Cards move through the board as work is planned, built, tested and evidenced.
